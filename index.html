In-vehicle safety,fully offline.

Featured production work: in-vehicle safety perception. A confidential production system, shown by architecture only.

The problem

Catch fatigue, distraction and safety violations in real time, with no network and no video leaving the vehicle.

What we built

Parallel vision models on one low-power chip, turning frames into graded safety events with on-device video clips and logs.

Engineering highlights

  1. Accuracy-preserving compression

    A calibrated low-precision pipeline that keeps on-device accuracy aligned with the full-precision model.

  2. Multi-model real-time scheduling

    Several concurrent networks sharing one low-power accelerator at stable frame rates.

  3. Train-to-device parity

    Numerical verification across the whole pipeline, so deployed outputs match what was validated in training.

  4. Temporal event reasoning

    Sequence modeling that turns noisy per-frame signals into reliable, severity-graded alerts.

  5. Offline-first reliability

    Self-contained capture, logging and evidence retention with zero cloud dependency.
